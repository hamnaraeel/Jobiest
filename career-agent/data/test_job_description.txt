Machine Learning Engineer
Example Company -- Islamabad, Pakistan (Remote friendly)

About the role:
Example Company is building computer vision systems for medical imaging analysis.
We are looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to join our small applied ML team
and help take research prototypes into reliable, deployed systems.

Responsibilities:
- Develop and deploy deep learning models for computer vision tasks, particularly
  image segmentation and classification.
- Work with large medical imaging datasets, including preprocessing and augmentation
  pipelines.
- Collaborate with research staff to translate published architectures into
  production-ready implementations.
- Write and maintain training/evaluation pipelines and track experiment results.
- Participate in code review and contribute to the team's engineering practices.

Requirements:
- 1+ years of professional or research experience in machine learning or a closely
  related field.
- Strong hands-on experience with Python.
- Strong hands-on experience with PyTorch.
- Demonstrated experience with computer vision (e.g. segmentation, classification,
  or object detection).
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.

Preferred:
- Experience with transformer-based architectures (e.g. Swin Transformer, ViT) is a
  plus.
- Familiarity with Docker is nice to have.
- Experience with AWS is a bonus but not required.

Location: Islamabad, Pakistan. Remote work is supported for this role.

Example Company is an equal opportunity employer.
